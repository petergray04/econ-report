The week in one paragraph

The labor market re-accelerated in August — payrolls +162K against a 55K consensus, June–July revised up, and initial claims back under 200K — just as inflation proved sticky: headline CPI held at 3.4% with a hot 0.3% core print and PPI climbed to 5.4% on energy. That combination pushed the Fed to its first hike under Chair Warsh (+25 bp to 3.75–4.00% on Sep 16). Rate-sensitive housing is the casualty: builder sentiment hit a one-year low, starts and permits missed, and the 30-yr mortgage rate is back near 7% with the 10-yr near 5%. Manufacturing surveys remain in expansion (ISM 54.6) even as factory output stalled. Equities have looked through it, with the S&P 500 up 13.4% YTD.
